## How to read this table

- **MLE/Data Science & Benchmarks**: direct systems and evaluations such as MLE-bench, AIDE, R&D-Agent, MLE-STAR, ML-Master, MLGym, MLR-Bench and PaperBench.
- **AI Research Agent / AI Scientist**: ideation-to-experiment-to-writing systems.
- **Agent Workflow / Multi-Agent Optimization**: search over prompts, workflows, graphs, roles and topologies.
- **Agent Training / Tool / Memory Optimization**: RL/post-training, tool-use and experience reuse mechanisms.
- **Self-Evolving Agent**: self-modification, evolutionary search, world models and open-ended improvement.
