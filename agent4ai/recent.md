## Why these additions matter

Several of these works push Agent4AI beyond a simple **code → metric → retry** loop. In particular, Arbor explicitly models hypotheses and evidence over time; AlphaLab accumulates reusable research knowledge in a persistent playbook; automated harness adaptation optimizes the agent infrastructure itself; and EXP-Bench/AIRS-Bench evaluate increasingly complete research processes rather than isolated ML engineering subtasks.
